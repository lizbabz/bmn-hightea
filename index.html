High Tea with a Twist is an intimate, curated experience for mothers in the diaspora — where the elegance of British high tea meets the boldness, warmth, and soul of Afro-Caribbean culture. Think fine china alongside jollof arancini, hibiscus tea served in delicate teacups, and a room full of women who truly get it.

This is more than an event. It's a celebration of who we are, where we're from, and the community we're building — one seat at a time.
